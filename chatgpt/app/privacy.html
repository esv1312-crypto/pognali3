POGNALI Privacy Policy

POGNALI is designed to help users discover and participate in real-world events. The ChatGPT integration receives only the information needed to perform the requested event actions.

Information used

Event information may include title, date, time, place, category, age range, description, participant capacity, participation state, messages, invitations, and user-provided age when required by an event.

Location

POGNALI does not request or infer precise device location in this ChatGPT integration. Users provide event locations explicitly.

Data use

Data is used to provide event discovery, participation, invitations, event chat, and related functionality. We do not use this integration to build a social feed, follower graph, or passive engagement profile.

Deletion and support

For privacy questions or deletion requests, use the support channel associated with the app submission.
